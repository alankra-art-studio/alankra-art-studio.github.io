ABOUT

Discover the sacred art form that restores inner peace and clarity

Mandala

A Mandala is a geometric configuration of symbols. A mandala generally represents the spiritual journey, starting from outside to the inner core, through layers.

Mandalas are far more than just geometric patterns or shapes to colour in. Mandalas have served as a sacred symbol for centuries and represent wholeness, harmony, and the infinite nature of the universe.

They all have circular designs with repeating shapes and patterns that radiate from the center.

The Healing Power of Sacred Geometry

When you color a mandala, your mind enters a meditative state of active stillness. The symmetrical patterns guide your focus inward, quietening repetitive thoughts, lowering stress levels, and awakening your intuitive creativity.

"The mandala is the center. It is the exponent of all paths. It is the path to the center, to individuation and inner harmony."
— Carl Gustav Jung (Pioneer of Mandala Art Therapy)
